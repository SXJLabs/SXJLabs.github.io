Privacy Policy

App: Study Planner
Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

Study Planner does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location or any other personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What is stored on your device

Your tasks, dates, XP, titles and class routine are saved only in your device's local storage. We cannot see this information and it is never uploaded to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Website visit counter

When the Study Planner website is opened in a normal web browser, it sends an anonymous request to a third-party counting service (counterapi.dev) to add one to a visit total. No name, account or task data is sent. Like any internet request, the service may technically receive your IP address. The installed Android app does not send this request.

What we do not do

No advertising. No analytics or tracking SDKs. No selling or sharing of personal data. No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
